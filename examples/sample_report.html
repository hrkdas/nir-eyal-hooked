Synthetic "5 Whys" Churn Interrogations

Qualitative interviews simulating dropped-out cohort personas to extract root-cause emotional friction.

The Casual Novice — Dropped out at Day 0 (Root Itch: boredom)
Why 1

Q: Hello The Casual Novice. Our telemetry shows you abandoned the application during initial onboarding. Why did you leave?

A: "I clicked the link expecting a quick solution, but was immediately confronted with a heavy setup flow."

Diagnosis: Initial Action Barrier (Fogg Ability Threshold Exceeded)

Why 2

Q: Why did the setup flow feel so heavy?

A: "It demanded 1 input fields and forced account creation before I even saw what the tool actually did for me."

Diagnosis: Violation of Simplicity Sieve (Mental & Physical Effort Friction / TTV Delay)

Why 3

Q: Why didn't the potential benefit justify taking 2 minutes to fill out those fields?

A: "Because I didn't have proof of value yet. Every modern app promises the world, but asking for my credentials upfront felt like homework."

Diagnosis: Asymmetric Investment Demand (Demanding Investment before Variable Reward)

Why 4

Q: Why were you looking for an application like this in that exact moment?

A: "I was feeling overwhelmed and rushed. I needed an instant answer, not a new software project to manage."

Diagnosis: Misalignment with User Context & Cognitive Bandwidth

Why 5

Q: What was the core underlying emotional itch you were trying to solve?

A: "Deep down, I felt acute boredom. When your product added friction instead of soothing that discomfort, my subconscious reacted by closing the tab."

Diagnosis: ROOT CAUSE: Failure to relieve the internal trigger (boredom) within 3 seconds.

Recommended Fix: Implement progressive disclosure: Remove registration gate, provide an instant sandbox/preview, and defer account creation until user attempts to save their work (Stored Value).
