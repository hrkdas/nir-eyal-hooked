# Reference 02: Triggers — The Spark of Behavior

> *"Triggers cue the user to take action. External triggers start the loop; internal triggers sustain it."* — Nir Eyal

---

## 1. External Triggers (The Scaffolding)

External triggers communicate information directly into the user's sensory environment:

| Type | Examples | Cost / Dynamics | Strategic Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paid** | Search ads, Instagram ads, influencer sponsorships | High ongoing CAC | Acquiring initial users. Unsustainable for long-term retention. |
| **Earned** | Viral mentions, press, PR features, App Store picks | High creative labor; non-repeatable | Sudden acquisition spikes. Cannot be scheduled on demand. |
| **Relationship**| Social invites, team invites, "Share link with coworker" | Viral coefficient ($K > 1$) | Hypergrowth. Vulnerable to user backlash if overly aggressive. |
| **Owned** | App icon on home screen, push notifications, email digest | Negligible marginal cost | **The foundation of habit formation.** Prompts repeated loops. |

---

## 2. Internal Triggers (The Emotional Itch)

Internal triggers manifest automatically in the mind without any external prompt. They are anchored in **negative emotional discomfort**:
* **Boredom** $\rightarrow$ Reaching for YouTube, Reddit, TikTok.
* **Loneliness / Social Alienation** $\rightarrow$ Reaching for Instagram, WhatsApp.
* **Uncertainty / Ignorance** $\rightarrow$ Reaching for Google, Perplexity.
* **Workplace Insecurity / FOMO** $\rightarrow$ Reaching for Slack, LinkedIn.

> **Key Rule**: A product is only habitual when the occurrence of a negative emotional state automatically sparks the thought of your product as the fastest psychological painkiller.

---

## 3. The 5 Whys Diagnostic Method

To uncover the root internal trigger, interrogate five layers deep:
1. *Why do they use the product?* $\rightarrow$ "To check updates."
2. *Why do they need updates?* $\rightarrow$ "To know what teammates did."
3. *Why do they need to know what teammates did?* $\rightarrow$ "To make sure tasks don't stall."
4. *Why do they care if tasks stall?* $\rightarrow$ "Because delays make them look incompetent."
5. *Why do they fear looking incompetent?* $\rightarrow$ **Root Itch: Acute anxiety over job status and peer respect.**
